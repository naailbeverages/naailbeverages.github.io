# Mango intake for 2026 closes 12% above last season

date: 2026-06-02

> Contracted pulp from Multan and Rahim Yar Khan comes in ahead of plan despite a late start.


This year's mango pulp intake closed at 12% above the 2025 season, despite a start delayed
by roughly two weeks in the Rahim Yar Khan belt.

We contract pulp ahead of the season rather than buying spot, and this year's contracts
covered 68 growers across Multan and Rahim Yar Khan districts — nine more than last year.

## Why it matters for the product

Pulp bought on contract comes from the same orchards year after year, which is what keeps
the flavour of Naail Mango Nectar consistent from batch to batch. Spot-market pulp varies
far more in Brix and colour, and the difference is noticeable in the glass.

> Contracting early costs us flexibility on price. What it buys is a product that tastes the
> same in March as it did in July.

## Production plan

Peak nectar production runs June through September. We expect to hold 40% pulp content
across the full season with no reformulation, and to carry sufficient aseptic pulp stock
into the first quarter of 2027.
