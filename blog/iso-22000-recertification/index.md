# ISO 22000 recertified through 2028

date: 2026-04-21

> The Sheikhupura plant clears its three-year food safety recertification audit with no major findings.


The Sheikhupura plant has been recertified to ISO 22000:2018 following a three-day audit in
April, with the certificate now valid through August 2028.

The audit covered all six production lines, the in-house laboratory, incoming material
control and the snack hall. It closed with no major non-conformities and two minor
observations, both relating to documentation rather than process.

## Observations and response

1. **Retention sample log format** — the log did not consistently record the storage
   temperature at the point of filing. We have moved the log to a fixed template with
   temperature as a required field.
2. **Supplier re-approval intervals** — two concentrate suppliers were past their scheduled
   re-approval date. Both have since been re-approved and the review calendar is now on a
   quarterly reminder.

Both were closed out within thirty days of the audit.
