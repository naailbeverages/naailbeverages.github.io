# Third water line commissioned at Sheikhupura

date: 2026-07-14

> A third bottling line lifts drinking-water capacity to 60,000 bottles a day ahead of the summer peak.


We have commissioned a third drinking-water bottling line at the Sheikhupura plant, taking
water capacity from 38,000 to 60,000 bottles a day.

The line handles 500 ml and 1.5 L PET and runs off the same reverse-osmosis and UV train as
the existing two, with an added 0.2 micron final filter stage. Commissioning took eleven
weeks, and the line entered commercial production on 1 July.

## Why now

The expansion was driven by demand from Lahore and Gujranwala, where summer orders had been
running roughly 30% ahead of what the previous two lines could fill. Through May and June we
were allocating rather than filling orders, which is not a position we want to be in during
the months that matter most.

## What changes for distributors

- 500 ml case availability improves immediately in Punjab (North)
- 19 L returnable delivery in Sheikhupura and Lahore moves from thrice- to four-times
  weekly from August
- No change to trade prices this quarter

Distributors with questions about revised allocations should contact their regional manager.
