# WE ARE NAAIL FOOD & BEVERAGES. BOTTLING PUNJAB'S HARVEST SINCE 2011.

> A Pakistan-based food and beverage manufacturer in Sheikhupura, Punjab, producing juices, carbonated drinks, drinking water and packaged snacks for distributors across all four provinces.


The company began in 2011 as a single-line juice filling unit on Lahore Road, Sheikhupura,
set up to process the mango surplus that growers in Multan and Rahim Yar Khan struggled to
move during peak season. The first product was a 250 ml mango nectar in a returnable glass
bottle, sold to roughly forty retailers in Sheikhupura district.

Demand from Lahore's wholesale market pushed us to a PET line in 2014. Carbonated soft
drinks followed in 2016, drinking water in 2017, and our snack division — namkeen mixtures
and roasted chana — opened in 2018 in a separate hall on the same site.

Today the plant runs three shifts across 4.2 acres, employs 340 people, and ships to more
than 120 distributors in Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Balochistan.

## How we source

We buy fruit pulp under seasonal contracts rather than on the spot market, which keeps
prices predictable for growers and keeps our flavour profile consistent batch to batch.
Mango pulp comes from Multan and Rahim Yar Khan, citrus concentrate from Sargodha and
Bhalwal, and apple concentrate from Swat and Quetta.

## Quality and certification

Every batch is tested in our on-site laboratory before it leaves the plant, and every bottle
carries a laser code identifying its production date, line, shift and blending tank. The
plant holds PSQCA, HACCP, ISO 22000:2018, ISO 9001:2015 and Halal certification.

Water for every line passes reverse osmosis, UV treatment and a 0.2 micron final filter.
Retention samples from each batch are held for twelve months at controlled temperature.
