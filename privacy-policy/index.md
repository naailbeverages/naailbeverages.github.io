# Privacy Policy


> How Naail Food & Beverages handles information submitted through this website.

## What we collect

If you submit the enquiry form on this website, we receive the name, email address, company
name and message you provide. We do not collect payment information through this site.

## How we use it

Enquiry details are used only to respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, to assess a
distributor application. We do not sell contact details to third parties.

## Retention

Enquiry records are retained for twenty-four months, after which they are deleted unless a
commercial relationship has been established.

## Cookies

This site does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Any analytics, if enabled, are
configured to record aggregate page views only.

## Your rights

To request a copy of the information we hold about you, or to ask for it to be deleted,
write to **hello@naailbeverages.example**.

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always the one published
on this page.
